Shading marks the selected period where it overlaps the plotted dates. The four lines always use their fixed trailing windows and all saved history, independently of the averaging window used for the estimate.

Speed at month t = [cutoff(t) − cutoff(t − N months)] ÷ N, in cutoff days per bulletin month. Each N-month rate needs N + 1 consecutive dated observations. Current, Unavailable, and missing values leave gaps; negative rates mean net retrogression.

Rates describe historical cutoff movement, not applicant waiting times or guaranteed future speeds. The 30.4 reference is approximate calendar pace (365.25 ÷ 12).

Your selected pace continues.
