# EB Priority Date Calculator

Estimate when EB1, EB2, or EB3 Chart A and Chart B cutoff dates could reach your priority date, using average progression from a historical period you choose.

**[Open the calculator →](https://bidagu.github.io/ebCalculator/)**

1. Select your employment category, chargeability region, and priority date.
2. Choose an averaging window; the default is the latest **24 months**.
3. View the estimates and explore **Cutoffs & gaps**, **Monthly heatmap**, and **Rolling speed** plots.

Supports China mainland, India, and the rest of the world, with English and Simplified Chinese interfaces. Estimates start from the latest saved bulletin. Calculations run in your browser, and your inputs stay on your device.

Estimates are historical extrapolations, not predictions or determinations of filing or approval eligibility. Chart B projections assume a May–September closure: estimates in those months move to October. **This is a modeling assumption, not an official rule.**

To use it offline, download the repository and open `index.html` in your browser. No installation or build step is needed.
